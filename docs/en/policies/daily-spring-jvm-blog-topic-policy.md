# Daily Spring/JVM Blog Topic Policy

> Language: [한국어](../../kr/policies/daily-spring-jvm-blog-topic-policy.md) | [English](./daily-spring-jvm-blog-topic-policy.md)

This policy keeps the Spring/JVM study section specific, practical, and grounded in official references.

## Purpose

The study topic should help a learner write or review one focused backend learning note, not consume a broad curriculum block.

## Topic requirements

- One concrete Spring Boot, JVM, Kotlin, or backend runtime topic.
- Exactly the required number of blog title candidates when validated.
- PAAR-style outline fields when required by the validator.
- A follow-up topic that continues the learning path.

## Reference requirements

Use official or stable references that the validator allows. Avoid unrelated vendor architecture pages unless policy explicitly allows them.

## Avoided wording

Avoid oversized expressions such as complete guide, everything, full mastery, or fixed day-based curriculum wording.

## Review checklist

- Specific topic.
- Supported reference domain.
- Actionable outline.
- No fixed curriculum wording.
- No oversized title wording.
