# Code of Conduct

> Language: [한국어](../kr/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [English](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

Career Feed is a small open-source automation project for backend learning, news review, and career briefing workflows.

The community standard is practical: keep discussions respectful, evidence-based, and safe for beginners and maintainers.

## Our standard

- Treat contributors, maintainers, and users with patience and respect.
- Focus feedback on the work, the evidence, and the repository scope.
- Assume documentation readers may be running a fork for the first time.

## Expected behavior

- Explain reproduction steps, source context, and expected results clearly.
- Use inclusive language and avoid personal attacks.
- Keep issues and pull requests scoped to Career Feed workflows, documents, configs, scripts, and validation policy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or repeated hostile comments are not acceptable.
- Publishing private information, webhook URLs, tokens, or personal contact details is not acceptable.
- Pressuring maintainers to bypass validation, security policy, or review boundaries is not acceptable.

## Project-specific expectations

Career Feed uses generated briefs and public source review. Suggestions should include source links, context, and why the item helps Java/Kotlin backend learners.

## Respect for beginners

Beginner questions are allowed when they are specific and safe to answer in public. Prefer concrete next steps over vague criticism.

## Respect for maintainers

Maintainers may decline broad rewrites, speculative features, or source suggestions that are hard to verify. A declined suggestion is not a personal judgment.

## Source and suggestion etiquette

- Prefer official, public, stable sources.
- Do not submit sources that require scraping private pages, bypassing paywalls, or collecting personal data.
- Label regional or language-specific suggestions clearly.

## Automation safety

Do not request automation that comments on external repositories, assigns issues, changes labels, stores secrets in files, or sends Discord messages during dry-run review.

## Reporting concerns

Report behavior concerns through maintainer contact paths when available. Do not include private personal information in public issues.

## Enforcement approach

Maintainers may edit, close, or lock discussions that violate this code. Severe or repeated violations may lead to blocked participation.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and project communication channels linked from the repository.

## Maintainer notes

Apply this policy consistently. Prefer a short explanation and a clear next step when moderating a contribution.
